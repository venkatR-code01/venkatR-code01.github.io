Phase 1 in progress started Nov 2026

Medical image AI

An AI that looks at medical images and explains what it sees in plain words. I'm building it in phases.

  1. Phase 1 · In progress Know the blood cell Upload a microscope image of one blood cell. The model tells you which of 8 blood cell types it is and explains it simply. It runs in your browser, so the image stays on your device.
  2. Phase 2 · Next Spot unusual cells Flag cells that look unusual, as research toward early signs of blood conditions such as leukemia, a blood cancer.
  3. Phase 3 · Later Scan reader Explore how AI could help read MRI and CT scans faster.
Planned stack
  • Python
  • EfficientNet
  • ONNX Runtime Web
  • AWS Lambda
  • Amazon Bedrock

Trained on open-source medical image data. A research project, not a medical tool: it never gives a diagnosis.
